# Annotated exemplar — Muñoz, "From Rights to Prerogatives" (the self-graded signpost)

## Source
- **Author / Work:** Daniel Muñoz, *From Rights to Prerogatives*, Philosophy and
  Phenomenological Research 102(3), 2021 · munozPrerogatives (verify against the source before citing)
- **Why this passage:** six words that rank the dialectic for the reader —
  transition, triage, and momentum in one stroke.

## The passage
> So much for the easy objection. Now the real challenges.

## What makes it work (the lesson)
- **It grades the objections publicly.** Telling the reader which objections are
  easy and which are real is a confidence move: it shows the author knows the
  difference — and licenses spending pages on the hard ones after dispatching
  the easy one briskly.
- **Two fragments, no connective tissue.** The transition *is* the sentence;
  compare the flabby alternative ("Having addressed this initial concern, I now
  turn to more serious difficulties…") — same content, triple the words, none of
  the snap.
- Works once or twice per paper, at the pivot from ground-clearing to the main
  dialectical event.

## What to borrow
When moving from minor to major objections, say so in one breath: dispatch, rank,
advance — "So much for X. Now the real challenge(s)." No throat-clearing.
