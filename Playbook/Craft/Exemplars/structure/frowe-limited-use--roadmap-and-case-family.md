# Annotated exemplar — Frowe, "The Limited Use View of the Duty to Save" (name the view in sentence one, then a section-by-section roadmap; minimal-variant named case family)

## Source
- **Author / Work:** Helen Frowe, "The Limited Use View of the Duty to Save," in *Oxford Studies in Political Philosophy* Volume 7, ed. Sobel, Vallentyne, and Wall (Oxford University Press, 2021): 67–. · suggested cite key: `frowe2021limited` (verify against the source before citing)
- **Why this passage:** Sentence one states the named thesis flat-out; the introduction then walks section by section ("In Section 2 … In Section 3 …"); and the body builds a family of cases that share a frame but vary one feature (Self-Help Sharks → Rescue Sharks).

## The passage
> This chapter defends the Limited Use View of the duty to save. The Limited Use View holds that the duty to save is a duty to treat oneself … as a means of preventing harm to others. … In Section 2, I develop the Limited Use View … In Section 3, I consider the saving of special others. In Section 4, I explore … the distinction between failing to save and depriving others of the means of saving themselves.

## What makes it work (the lesson)
- **Thesis is named, then defined, in the first three sentences.** "defends the Limited Use View" — a proper-named position — followed immediately by a one-sentence gloss of what it holds. The reader knows the destination before any argument starts.
- **The roadmap is a literal contract.** "In Section 2 … In Section 3 …" tells the referee exactly where each promissory note is paid. It also previews the *dialectic* (a rival view appears, then a dilemma for it), not just the table of contents.
- **A named case family, not isolated examples.** *Self-Help Sharks* and *Rescue Sharks* keep the threat structure (Small Shark/one leg vs. Big Shark/two legs) fixed and vary only who is positioned to act, so the contrast in verdicts is attributable to that single change.
- **The view is scoped as it is stated** ("limited … one need not treat oneself as a means when doing so is either very costly or conflicts with one's more stringent duties") — the qualifications are baked into the thesis, not bolted on later.

## What to borrow
State your position by its proper name in the first sentence, gloss it in the next, give a numbered "In Section n, I …" roadmap that previews the dialectic, and build your cases as a minimal-variant family sharing a fixed frame.
