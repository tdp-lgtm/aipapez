BLIND REFEREE TEMPLATES

Rules that make a prompt genuinely blind:
- Give the referee ONLY: its disciplinary lens, the venue, and the manuscript file path.
- NEVER name the draft's own design features, arguments, or terminology in the prompt
  (naming doctrines/methods that define the LENS is fine; naming what the PAPER does is not).
- Fresh agent instance every round; never show prior reports.
- Round 2+ adds one case-for-acceptance reader (articles-editor persona, argues FOR the piece).
- Readiness check = the committee-hygiene read + one generalist publishability read.

PER-PAPER SLOTS are marked {LIKE THIS} in each template. Adjust ONLY those slots:
{VENUE}, {LENS FIELD}, {LENS DOCTRINE LIST} (the bodies of law/literatures the lens
brings — NOT the paper's uses of them), {MANUSCRIPT PATH}.

Track convergence across rounds in the paper's Referee Reports folder: one line per
concern, which rounds raised it, cleared/open. Convergence across fresh readers is the
readiness signal; a concern no fresh reader repeats after a fix is cleared.
