# Argument Clinic — Stage 4C pass (on Fat Outline v0.2)

Run 2026-09-22, fresh read from file. Target: central argument (4.8 display, defended §3–4) + held concessions (6.2 close, 6.4, 6.6).

## Lead finding (most serious, stated plainly)

**The ignored-claim point, as written, condemns prophetic coinages.** §3.9 makes "minting into predictable non-uptake" a second-order wronging. But many just claims face predictable non-uptake because audiences unjustly refuse them — early feminist and abolitionist concepts were coined into predictable refusal and were paradigm goods. Worse, the practice's own literature names this refusal (contributory injustice). As written, P3 lets a hostile audience's foreseeable refusal count against the *coiner*. That is both a counterexample and a moral embarrassment, and a specialist referee finds it in minutes.

**Repair (accepted):** distinguish *answerability* from *actual uptake*. The wrong the ignored-claim point identifies is minting claims that could not be answered *even by a willing audience* — non-uptake grounded in the coinage's own structure (no capacity, no mechanism, no shape: *Ambient Injustice*). Non-uptake grounded in audience refusal of an answerable claim is the audience's wrong, not the coiner's — indeed it is the practice's own category of contributory injustice. Clause (iii) accordingly demands an *articulable* response-shape, not existing institutions willing to respond. This repair (a) saves P3 from the counterexample, (b) hardens the §6 equity reply (the condition cannot blame victims of uptake-refusal — refusal presupposes answerability), (c) yields a crisper gloss: **backing is answerability**. Fixed in outline v0.3: 3.9, 4.4 gloss, 6.4, 6.6.

## Reconstruction (charitable, from 4.8)

- P1. To coin "K-injustice" (suffix-coinage, thick use) is to mint a claim-kind: a standing form under which members of K's extension can claim. [§3.1–3.2]
- P2. Claim-kinds spend no deontic validity: recognizing new wrongs does not thin the wrongness of old ones. [§3.3]
- P3. Claim-kinds draw on uptake, which is scarce and owed. [§3.4–3.8]
- P4. A backed coinage economizes the scarce resource; an unbacked one spends it. [§4.5–4.7]
- C. Proliferation is justified when and because its coinages are backed.

Reconstruction matches the outline's display. Two suppressed premises found:

- **SP1 (normative bridge):** "investing in a scarce, morally significant commons is pro tanto justified; drawing it down without return is pro tanto unjustified." Without it, P4→C doesn't close. Also, an unbacked coinage might be justified by other goods (expressive value, solidarity, future backability). **Fix (accepted):** state C as pro tanto in the 4.8 display; the scope fence ("comparative and defeasible") already anticipates this — now the display says it. Fixed in v0.3.
- **SP2 (tendency claim inside P4):** "the uptake capacity a backed coinage adds exceeds its own adoption costs." Surfaced by the congestion objection below.

## Premise-by-premise

- **P1** — Sound for the essay's topic (suffix-coinages, thick use); the 3.2 micro-concession fences thin uses. A referee might ask whether concept-introduction or labeling mints the claim-kind; §5.5 (label corollary) answers: the suffix. Consistent. No change.
- **P2** — Strong; best attempted counterexample: imperfect duties of aid, where more recognized claimants do thin per-claimant fulfillment. But that is fulfillment (uptake side), not bindingness — the case *supports* the validity/uptake distinction rather than breaking it. **Optional enrichment (accepted, cheap):** one sentence in 3.3 using the imperfect-duty case as a bridge to 3.4. Fixed in v0.3.
- **P3** — Two findings. (i) **Equivocation on "uptake" (clear gap):** 3.2 defines uptake as what a claim calls for (demand side, normative); 3.4–3.5 use it as finite processing (supply side, descriptive). Same word, two roles — a foundations referee will pounce. **Fix (accepted):** two terms — *uptake* (the recognition-and-response a claim calls for; owed) vs *uptake capacity* (the finite social ability to supply it; scarce). Scarcity claims attach to capacity, owed-ness to uptake. Fixed in v0.3 (3.2 definition; usage discipline note for drafting). (ii) The prophetic-coinage counterexample — lead finding, repaired via answerability. Also: **wording discipline (accepted):** 3.6 must not imply Smith & Archer establish claim-directed duties; they establish person-directed attention-justice; the claim-directed normativity rides on the internal turn (3.8). Noted for drafting.
- **P4** — **The congestion objection (clear gap; the strongest remaining attack):** backing is assessed coinage-by-coinage, but the commons problem is aggregate. Even individually backed categories can jointly congest — taxonomy bloat: a triage system with 40 headings works, with 40,000 collapses. So "backed ⇒ economizes" fails in aggregate; SP2 exposed. **Fix (accepted, three-part reply as new 4.7b):** (a) backed coinages *nest* — the mechanism clause locates each new kind relative to existing kinds (the actual family is a tree: species under the epistemic genus), so backed proliferation extends a taxonomy rather than flatly multiplying headings, and nesting is precisely what unbacked coinages cannot do (no mechanism, no location); (b) uptake capacity is not fixed — backed coinages historically create capacity (training, procedures; already 4.5); (c) concession with scope: an absorption limit exists in principle; the actual practice (dozens of coinages) is nowhere near it, and the Investment View prices marginal coinages, which is the live question. Fixed in v0.3.
- **Concessions audit:** 6.2-close and 6.4 consistent with the answerability repair (strengthened by it). 6.6's "unbackable today may become backable" risked colliding with the prophetic point (institutions' current willingness must not decide backedness) — **fix (accepted):** articulability, not institutional readiness, is the clause-(iii) standard; 6.6's mobility concerns shifts in what is articulable. Fixed in v0.3.

## Strongest objection (built better than the opponent would)

Congestion, final form: "Your own economics defeats you. Scarcity is aggregate; your condition is marginal. Each backed coinage passes your test while the sum congests triage — the tragedy of the commons survives clause-by-clause virtue. Your slogan should read: minting is not spending, until everyone mints." Reply as 4.7b above: nesting (structural), capacity-creation (historical), and the priced-margin concession (scope). The reply defeats the objection for the practice as it exists and concedes the in-principle limit — an honest fallback that still delivers C.
