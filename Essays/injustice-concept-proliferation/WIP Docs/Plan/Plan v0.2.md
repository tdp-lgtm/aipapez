## 1. The thesis, sharpest form

**Compressed (as it will appear on p.1):** The proliferation of injustice concepts is neither debasement nor free bounty. Coining an injustice concept draws on a scarce public resource — our collective capacity to take claims up — and it is justified when, and because, the coinage is *backed*: when it fixes who is wronged in what capacity, by what mechanism, and what would count as a response. Backed coinages do not spend the commons; they enlarge it. The flagship coinages are backed. So the practice, as actually conducted, has been good for philosophy and for society — but what keeps it good is the condition, not the momentum.

**Named view:** the **Investment View**. Named criterion: the **Backing Condition**. Named rivals: the **Inflation View** (proliferation devalues injustice-talk; the rights-inflation critique transposed) and the **Abundance View** (concepts are non-rival and coining is costless, so proliferation needs no license).

**One-sentence test:** I argue that injustice-concept proliferation is good when and because coinages are backed — and that the practice's exemplars are backed — against the Inflation View and the Abundance View alike, on the grounds that injustice claims compete for scarce uptake rather than scarce validity, and a backed coinage economizes uptake rather than spending it.

**Scope sentences (stated in §1):** I do not claim that anyone should be stopped from coining; the Backing Condition is a standard of success, not a licensing regime. I do not claim that unbacked coinage names no real bad — only that the *injustice* frame is not yet earning its keep there. And my verdict on the practice is comparative and defeasible, not a blanket blessing of every future coinage.

## 2. Why this is the sharpest version (and not just a version)

- It concedes to each rival exactly what is true in it (Inflation: scarcity is real; Abundance: validity is non-rival), so the essay's contribution is located precisely at the join: *what* is scarce, and *how* coinages relate to it. That join is unoccupied (Literature Map §D–E).
- Weaker variants: "proliferation is good if concepts are good" (trivial); "attention matters" (Kitsik already there); "adopt quality control" (Alston's procedural point, in the wrong domain). The sharp version adds: the scarcity is internal to justice-practice (ignored claims are further wrongings), and backedness *reverses the sign* of a coinage's draw on the commons — the economizing step no one has.
- The strong modality is avoided: "when and because backed" + "the exemplars are backed" gives a positive verdict without claiming every coinage is good or that goodness is guaranteed.

## 3. The analytic move (explicit)

Three steps, all mine to make:
1. **Claim-theoretic step:** injustice-ascription is claim-making (Feinberg's claiming activity, extended from rights to injustice-kind ascriptions): to call a situation K-injustice is to assert that someone's treatment demands response, not merely to sort it. This is what distinguishes the "-injustice" suffix from neutral taxonomy — and why the Abundance View's non-rivalry premise, true of *concepts*, is false of *claims*.
2. **Internalized-scarcity step:** uptake (recognition + response) is scarce; and within justice-practice this scarcity is not mere friction: a valid claim that predictably meets no uptake leaves its bearer wronged twice. So a practice that mints claim-kinds affects existing claimants — the externality the Inflation View gestures at, stated without the currency metaphor (whose failures Theilen documents in the rights case).
3. **Economizing step (the reversal):** a backed coinage functions like a good diagnostic category — it bundles detection, address, and remedy-shape, cutting the per-instance cost of uptake for every future case (testimonial injustice made a recurrent clinical and legal failure recognizable *and actionable* at a glance). So backed minting is investment in the uptake commons; only unbacked minting is spending. The critics' own scarcity premise, granted in full, yields a conditional vindication of proliferation. (Internal-objections architecture: the rival defeated by its own lights.)

## 4. Structural moves selected (from the Moves Catalog, Part 1)

1. **Paired-cases opening:** two coinages, both naming genuine bads, one manifestly earning its keep (real: testimonial injustice) and one constructed to be idle (*Idle Coinage* case, built in §4A outline; design constraint: the bad must be socially caused — so the "mere misfortune" reply is blocked — yet the coinage specifies no capacity, no mechanism, no redress-shape). The pair does the framing: the reader's diverging verdicts can only track backedness.
2. **The iteratively revised principle:** the Backing Condition is built in public — simple version ("a coinage is justified iff it names a real wrong") broken by *Idle Coinage*, repaired clause by clause, with the structural-injustice worry forcing the final form of clause (iii) (redress-shape, not culprit — Young).
3. **Internal objections / grant-the-conditional:** against the Inflation View, grant scarcity, deny devaluation-by-minting; against the Abundance View, grant concept-non-rivalry, deny claim-non-rivalry.

## 6. Dialectical structure

- **Preemptive caveats (§1):** not a licensing regime; not ranking wrongs; not anti-Fricker — the view vindicates the practice's exemplars.
- **Interleaved micro-objections:** §3 "attention doesn't follow concepts" (Kitsik's mechanisms answer); §4 vacuity/toothlessness (answered by exhibited failure); §5 "ontic injustice has no redress-shape" (worked in place — it does: recognition-and-reconstruction of the kind).
- **Dedicated section (§6):** the three cross-cutting objections above; structural gets most space because it attacks the criterion's most distinctive clause.
- **Concessions ledger (planned):** to Inflation — scarcity real, and *some* coinage behavior is genuinely extractive; to Abundance — concepts are non-rival, and gatekeeping proposals (Alston-style licensing) overshoot; to Shklar — the misfortune/injustice line moves, which is why backing is a condition on *coinages*' current serviceability, not a metaphysical boundary.
