## Kitsik 2025 (closest neighbor) — read in full
- Her topic confirmed: *inflation* = widening of problem-categories via changed linguistic practice ("easier to indicate a problem of a certain category"). NOT the coining of new kind-concepts. She even names coining new terms as the benign alternative: "We can... come up with and secure uptake for new snappy words, instead of incorporating all related... problems under the same existing category" (§3). → The essay's question is the natural successor to hers; proliferation/creep distinction survives and is sharpened: her account IS the creep side.
- Her verdict-shape: communication and understanding worries fail (Liao & Hansen flexibility; anti-linguistic-determinism via Barber & Stainton; Sliwa's aptness-of-perspective); the real stake is collective attention allocation; whether to support a given widening reduces to first-order political judgment of the problems' importance, where "important enough" = "important for achieving what currently ought to be our shared goals" (§5). No adequacy condition on introduction; no claim-theoretic step; expressly neutral.
- HER MECHANISMS (§4): *emotion* (mobilizing emotional impact of the terms; with reliability caveats — disgust/defensiveness can backfire) and *commitment* (individuals/institutions publicly committed to addressing category K must prioritize whatever falls under K; explains funding/research reallocation; "backed up by public vows and cannot be changed without a backlash"). → Cite for 3.7's channel claim; the commitment mechanism is the citable engine of *The Docket*.
- Attention scarcity: James quote ("withdrawal from some things in order to deal effectively with others"); "even a collective cannot prioritize everything of significance"; Gardiner 2022 on collective attentional resources (funding, space, time, column inches) — cite Gardiner via Kitsik's presentation or request original (added to REQUESTS).
- Relation sentence, refined: Kitsik locates the stake and hands the verdict to first-order politics; the essay adds a *form-level*, content-neutral condition prior to prioritization — an unbacked coinage cannot even enter the priority negotiation as an answerable claim-kind.
- New leads (added to REQUESTS): Maitra 2018 "New Words for Old Wrongs" (Episteme — title adjacent to our distinction; MUST check before final originality re-check); Liao & Hansen 2023 JAPA; Cabezas 2024 Ethics; Tasioulas 2021 Aeon; Gardiner 2022.

## Jenkins 2020 (ontic injustice; §5.3 trial) — read in full
- Definition: wronged by being socially constructed as member of a social kind whose construction consists partly in wrongful constraints/enablements. Wrong = Hampton's *moral injury* (damage to acknowledgement of value via diminishment); backup account for skeptics = pure risk-imposition. Capacity clause fill: the subject as bearer of moral entitlements/recognition respect (Darwall). Mechanism clause fill: the mismatch between moral entitlements and kind-constituting constraints/enablements.
- REDRESS-SHAPE — explicit and two-track (§5): contingent sites → reform the kind (wife post-1991: marital rape exemption ended, kind changed, label survives); necessary sites → abolish the kind (slave). §6: the concept's claim "can function as the basis for a demand for intervention to change the nature of these kinds"; Haslanger's "levers for change." → Clause (iii) PASSES resoundingly; the trial can exhibit both tracks.
- Bonus: her §6 opens by explicitly answering "what do we gain by having the concept?" — "earn its philosophical keep" is HER phrase (do not echo it as ours; cite or rephrase). Her intro names the genre (Fricker, Kukla) — usable for 1.3 boom evidence.

## Dotson 2012 — read in full
- Contributory injustice: willful hermeneutical ignorance + structurally prejudiced resources thwart contribution; the marginalized CAN articulate (in their own resources); what fails is uptake by dominant-resource users. → Direct grounding for the answerability/refusal distinction (3.9): the practice itself locates such refusal as the AUDIENCE's injustice.
- Her critique of Fricker: the epistemic-bad-luck boundary is a *closed conceptual structure* that forecloses further forms and thereby perpetrates epistemic oppression; remedy: *open conceptual structures* ("an account", not "the account"); "a catchall theory of epistemic injustice is an unrealistic expectation."
  → Sharpens the equity objection (6.3): a backing NORM looks like boundary-policing. And equips the reply (6.4): B3 is open in exactly her sense — it defines no boundary of injustice, conditions coinages one-by-one, is satisfiable in indefinitely many ways, and blocks the Fricker-style move (no coinage is excluded by a prior taxonomy; each carries its own answerability).
- Her order-of-change heuristic (first/second/third-order changes required to address each form) = redress-shape methodology practiced explicitly — the three forms "can be shown to be distinct forms by identifying the kinds of changes each minimally requires." → Cite in 5.6/6.4: the practice's own best work distinguishes coinages by remedy-shape.

## Feinberg 1970 — read in full
- Nowheresville; claiming as rule-governed activity; performative vs propositional claiming; "having rights enables us to 'stand up like men'"; "human dignity may simply be the recognizable capacity to assert claims." Anchor for 3.1.
- **Manifesto rights (new, important):** claims-*to* without valid claims-*against*; Fawcett: "claims are needs and demands in movement... continuous transformation of claims into rights"; Feinberg licenses manifesto-talk as "permanent possibilities of rights," determinants of *present* aspirations. → Two uses: (a) supports clause (iii)'s shape-not-respondent form and the prophetic-coinage protection (claims can validly outrun assignable addressees); (b) supplies an objection: doesn't manifesto-license legitimate unbacked coinages as rhetoric? Reply: the license is earned by the determinacy of the *need* and the direction of transformation — manifesto claims fix capacity and mechanism and point their movement; *Ambient Injustice* fixes nothing and moves nowhere. ADD to outline (4.4 or 3.9 + footnote).
