## §3 What a Coinage Spends (1,200) — CRUX SECTION

3.1 The claim-making analysis: injustice-ascription as claiming (Feinberg's activity extended from rights to injustice-kinds); the suffix's verdictive force = what distinguishes it from neutral taxonomy. Display: none here; definition of *uptake* as the pair (recognition, response).
3.2 First result, against naive Inflation: deontic validity is not scarce (new duties don't dilute old ones; the *Two Duties* micro-case). Grant-the-conditional move: if coinages spent validity, dilution would follow; they don't.
3.3 Second result, against Abundance: what IS scarce — uptake. Kitsik's finding as the hinge (communication/understanding unaffected; collective attention the live stake) (verify — awaiting #1); Smith & Archer as independent normative support (attention can be owed) (verify — awaiting #5).
3.4 Micro-objection interleaved: "attention allocation doesn't track concept-count" — answer via the mechanisms (concepts are how claims become countable, teachable, litigable; institutional uptake runs on categories) (verify — awaiting #1 for Kitsik's mechanism inventory).
3.5 The ignored-claim point (the essay's own load-bearing move): claims demand response; a claim-kind minted into predictable non-uptake manufactures claimants wronged twice. So uptake scarcity is internal to justice-practice, not external friction. This is where the crux from the sketch is solved.
3.6 Upshot display-free summary + pivot: coinages differ in how they relate to the commons; §4 builds the test.

Exit state: the crux (what do coinages compete for?) settled: uptake, and morally, not merely causally.

## §4 The Backing Condition (1,050)

4.1 Iterative construction, version-numbered in public:
  - B0: "a coinage is justified iff it names a real wrong" → broken by *Idle Coinage* (real bad, idle concept).
  - B1: add *capacity* clause (wronged as what?) → broken by a case with capacity but no mechanism (variant case).
  - B2: add *mechanism* clause → broken by structural-wrong worry (no culprit) → clause (iii) formulated as *redress-shape*, not culprit.
  - B3 = the Backing Condition, final, as the essay's single labeled display.
4.2 The economizing step: backed coinage as diagnostic bundling (detection + address + redress-shape per instance); the reversal stated: backed minting invests, unbacked minting spends. Slogan (flagged): "Minting is not spending; unbacked minting is."
4.3 Micro-objection interleaved: vacuity ("everything passes") — answered by exhibiting *Idle Coinage* failing each clause, and by noting the condition has teeth precisely because it can fail.
4.4 Central argument displayed ONCE in premise form (house rule): P1 claim-making (§3.1), P2 validity non-scarcity (§3.2), P3 uptake scarcity + ignored-claim point (§3.3–3.5), P4 economizing (§4.2), C conditional vindication + verdict schema. Defense locations named.

Exit state: the test exists, is motivated, and the central argument is on the page in auditable form.

## §6 Objections (900)

6.1 The structural objection (longest): clause (iii) seems to demand what structural injustice cannot give; reply via Young's social connection model — redress-shape as forward-looking shared responsibility; concession: where even that shape is absent, the coinage is not yet backed — and that is the right verdict. (verify — awaiting #9.)
6.2 The genus objection (Sketch B's ghost): verdictive labels need a theory of injustice, not a checklist; reply: the Backing Condition IS the operational genus-commitment (each clause forces the coiner to say what makes this *injustice*); engages Byskov (the field already polices the suffix) and concedes to Shklar that the misfortune/injustice line moves — which is why backing conditions serviceability now, not metaphysics forever. (verify — awaiting #11, #21.)
6.3 The reflexivity objection (disarm): the Backing Condition is itself a normative coinage-adjacent proposal — does it pass its own test? Show it does (capacity: claimants generally; mechanism: unbacked minting; redress-shape: the condition itself) — and that the test's cheapness proves the view non-censorious.

Exit state: strongest attack absorbed with a real concession; two rivals' residual appeal explained.
