## §2 Two Views About Minting

- 2.1 Distinction minted: **proliferation** (new kind-concepts added) vs **creep** (an existing concept's extension stretched — Haslam's horizontal/vertical expansion (verify — #6)). Sub-argument: their economics differ — creep alters the backing of an existing concept (its mechanism clause blurs as cases pile in); proliferation adds concepts whose backing is assessable at birth. Example: "trauma" stretched (creep) vs "ontic injustice" coined (proliferation). Payoff: critics' evidence is mostly creep-evidence; the boom is proliferation — so the standard critique misses its target unless restated. (This restatement is a service to the critics, not a strawman: steelman follows.)
- 2.2 Inflation View steelmanned, 3 steps: (a) Alston: uncontrolled announcement of new rights erodes the currency's credibility — quality control needed (verify — #2); (b) Griffin: inflationary pressure debases the term's determinateness (verify — #10); (c) transposition: injustice-talk as claim-currency; every coinage is an issue of new denominations; unbacked issues devalue all. Close with what is right: the scarcity intuition and the reality of extractive coinage behavior (academic incentive to mint). Placed objection (answered §3.2): the currency model itself.
- 2.3 Abundance View steelmanned, 3 steps: (a) concepts are non-rival public goods — one person's use doesn't deplete another's; (b) Fricker's framework makes gap-filling a *matter of justice* (hermeneutical lacunae harm; coining remedies) (verify — #3); (c) Nikolaidis: proliferation plus critical tools serves flourishing (verify — #8) — note his rider concedes a filter is needed, which the essay will cash as backing. Close with what is right: non-rivalry of concepts; wrongness of licensing regimes. Placed objection (answered §3.3–3.5): "nothing scarce is drawn on."
- 2.4 Impasse diagnosis: the camps disagree about the *object* of expenditure without naming one. The essay's question sharpened: what, if anything, does a coinage spend? (Pivot.)

## §3 What a Coinage Spends — CRUX

- 3.1 Claim-making analysis, 3 sub-arguments:
  - (a) Practice observation: coinage papers do not merely classify — they call for recognition, reform, redress (their own closing sections say so) (verify — check against #1, #4, #7 full texts for fair characterization).
  - (b) Feinberg extension: claiming is the activity that gives rights their value (Nowheresville) (verify — #20); injustice-ascription is claiming's kind-level analogue: to coin "K-injustice" is to mint a claim-*kind* — a standing form for claims by anyone in the kind's extension.
  - (c) Minimal contrast: "epistemic bad luck" vs "epistemic injustice" — same events sortable, only the latter demands response; the suffix carries verdictive, claim-making force. Definition display-free: **uptake** = (recognition, response) owed to a claim.
  - Micro-concession: some tokens of the suffix are thin/classificatory; the analysis targets the practice's central, thick use.
- 3.2 Against naive Inflation — validity is not scarce:
  - (a) *Two Wrongs*: recognizing the torture of B as wrong subtracts nothing from the wrongness of torturing A; bindingness doesn't dilute with recognition of more wrongs. (Case built to avoid resource-conflictable duties — no time/means competition in the vignette.)
  - (b) Grant-the-conditional: IF coinages spent validity, dilution would follow; they don't, so the currency model fails as stated — and Theilen's deconstruction of rights-inflation rhetoric corroborates in the home domain (verify — #15).
  - (c) Diagnose the rival's appeal: the inflation idiom survives because *something* nearby is scarce; the critics reached for the wrong scarce thing. (Sets up 3.3.)
- 3.3 What is scarce — uptake, 3 sub-arguments:
  - (a) Attention finite (Simon anchor, one line (verify — #13)); institutional processing finite: dockets, curricula, complaint procedures, news cycles.
  - (b) *The Docket*: an ombuds office runs k complaint categories; adding category k+1 changes triage for every filed claim — not because anyone's claim got less valid, but because processing is rationed. Verdict: uptake competition is structural, not psychological speculation.
  - (c) Uptake can be *owed*: Smith & Archer — attention deficits as epistemic injustice (verify — #5); so allocation of uptake is itself justice-apt (not mere throughput). Kitsik's hinge: communication/understanding largely robust under conceptual change; collective attention the live stake (verify — #1). Note the essay's relation to Kitsik stated in one honest sentence: she locates the stake; this essay normativizes it and derives a condition.
- 3.4 Micro-objection: "attention doesn't track concept-count":
  - Reply mechanisms — categories are how claims become (i) perceptible at a glance, (ii) countable/aggregable (reports, statistics), (iii) filable (complaints need names), (iv) teachable (curricula). (Align with Kitsik's mechanism inventory where her text supports; otherwise own argument (verify — #1).)
  - Concession: the mapping is loose at the margin; the argument needs only that coinages are a *non-trivial channel* of uptake allocation, not the sole determinant. (Built-in fallback move.)
- 3.5 The ignored-claim point (load-bearing, own move), 2 sub-arguments + internal turn:
  - (a) Internal turn: the practice's founding concept already treats uptake failure as injustice — testimonial injustice IS wrongful recognition-failure (verify — #3). So by the practice's own lights, a valid claim met with predictable non-uptake wrongs its bearer again.
  - (b) Minting into predictable non-uptake therefore manufactures second-order wronging: the coinage invites claims it cannot help answer. *Ambient Injustice* claimants: told they are wronged, given nothing that makes response possible.
  - Placed objection: "proves too much — all moral discourse competes for attention" → reply: only verdictive coinages mint claim-kinds; analysis, description, theory-building do not demand uptake; the suffix does. (Also seeds §5.5.)
- 3.6 Upshot: coinages spend or invest uptake; which one depends on a property of the coinage. Pivot to §4.

## §4 The Backing Condition

- 4.1 Iterated principle (public repairs; version labels B0–B3; *Ambient Injustice* family as the breaking cases):
  - B0 "justified iff it names a real bad" — broken by *Ambient Injustice* (bad is real; coinage idle).
  - B1 + capacity clause ("fixes whom it wrongs, as what") — broken by *Ambient Injustice\** (adds "wronged as residents of modernity" — capacity without mechanism still yields unfilable claims).
  - B2 + mechanism clause ("the route by which the arrangement wrongs the capacity") — pressured by structural wrongs (no culprit) — repair: the response clause must not demand a perpetrator.
  - B3 final, the essay's one labeled display: a coinage "K-injustice" is **backed** iff it fixes (i) *capacity* — who is wronged, as what; (ii) *mechanism* — the route of the wronging; (iii) *redress-shape* — what responding to the claim would be, which may be shared and forward-looking rather than culprit-directed.
- 4.2 The economizing step, 2 sub-arguments:
  - (a) Diagnostic analogy worked once (governing image family: minting/backing/investment — one image system per catalog rule): a good diagnostic category bundles detection, address, and treatment-shape; per-instance cognitive and institutional cost of uptake falls. *Dismissed* revisited: the clinician's training now includes the pattern; recognition is cheap where it was impossible.
  - (b) The reversal: therefore backed minting *adds* uptake capacity (invests); only unbacked minting spends. The critics' scarcity premise, granted, flips sign under backing. Slogan (flagged, once): "Minting is not spending; unbacked minting is."
- 4.3 Vacuity micro-objection: answered by *Ambient Injustice* failing all three clauses in sequence (exhibited, not asserted), + the observation that clause-satisfaction is checkable in coinage papers' own texts (the good ones do it explicitly — evidence in §5).
- 4.4 Central argument, displayed once, premise form (defense locations): P1 (§3.1) coining K-injustice mints a claim-kind; P2 (§3.2) claim-kinds spend no validity; P3 (§3.3–3.5) claim-kinds draw on uptake, which is scarce and owed; P4 (§4.2) a backed coinage economizes uptake, an unbacked one spends it; C: proliferation is justified when and because coinages are backed — the practice's verdict is empirical, settled in §5.

## §6 Objections

- 6.1 Structural objection (longest; steelman then reply):
  - Steelman: Young shows structural injustice resists liability-model assignment (verify — #9); if clause (iii) demands assignable response, the most important coinages fail; the condition would be conservative in exactly the way Theilen warns inflation-talk is (verify — #15).
  - Reply: clause (iii) was built (B2→B3) to demand response-*shape*, not respondent: Young's social connection model IS a redress-shape (shared, forward-looking, political) — so structural coinages can pass; what cannot pass is a coinage for which even shape-level response is inarticulable.
  - Concession (bite the bullet, defend it): such maximally diffuse coinages are not yet backed, and that is the intuitively right verdict — cf. *Ambient Injustice*; the condition is met by Young-style theory, which is exactly the work the good structural theorists in fact do.
- 6.2 Genus objection: steelman from killed Sketch B (verdictive labels need grounds now — a theory of what makes all these *injustice* — not a checklist); reply in 2 steps: (a) the Backing Condition is the operational form of the genus-commitment — each clause forces a "wrongs-whom-how-answerable-how" statement, which is what having grounds *is* at the point of coining; (b) demanding a full genus theory first would be over-demanding in a way the practice's own history refutes (Byskov: the genus debate proceeds productively AFTER coining (verify — #11)); concede to Shklar: the misfortune/injustice line is historically mobile (verify — #21), which is why backing conditions present serviceability, not eternal metaphysics — the condition inherits her lesson instead of fighting it.
- 6.3 Reflexivity objection (short, disarming): does the Backing Condition back itself? It is not an injustice-coinage, but run the analogue: capacity — claimants at large, as claimants; mechanism — unbacked minting's manufacture of unanswerable claims; redress-shape — the condition itself (a naming norm anyone can apply). The test passes its own analogue and costs a coiner one paragraph to meet: cheap, non-censorious, self-consistent.
