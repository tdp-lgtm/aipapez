## 1. The landscape in one paragraph

The Means Principle (MP) says that harming someone as a means to others' good is harder to justify than harming her as a side effect. It has to explain a set of puzzling pairs: Switch and Bridge; the strategic and the terror bomber; Scarcity and Guinea Pig; Room and Heart Attack; Driftwood and Life Jacket. It is also meant to constrain lesser-evil justifications and agent-relative prerogatives in the ethics of defensive force, war, punishment, and strikes. See the functions F1–F6 in the Reading Notes' synthesis.

Since 2011 the literature has run along two tracks that rarely meet:
- **The criterion track** asks which relation between victim and good triggers the principle: Quong's absence test, Ramakrishnan's "in virtue of usefulness" relation, Walen's restricting claims and toolkit baseline, Øverland's moral obstacles, Kamm's causal-structure doctrines.
- **The rationale track** asks why any such relation should matter: Quinn's unowed service, Tadros's compulsion and enforceable duty, Alexander's jurisdiction, Quong's distributive justice, Cullity's recognitional respect. Two sceptics hold that there is no rationale at all: Ramakrishnan (the principle is probably primitive) and Steinhoff.

Each track has a standing failure that the other could repair. The best criterion, Ramakrishnan's, has no explanation and admits it looks like "mere causal structure." The best rationale, the duty-based family, is either too broad or circular, by Quong's dilemma, because it lacks an independent criterion.

That gap between the tracks is the niche.

### 2.3 Cross-cutting disputes the essay must engage

- **Omissions without prior rights.** Parry 2023 (file 06 L47–105): *Icy Trolley* is wrong, but on Quong's view Billy has no independent right to rescue (compare *Icy Trolleys*). So the MP must *inform* rights, and the test must find "some quasi-causal difference ... more fine-grained than counterfactual dependence" to keep *Room* apart (L105). Tadros 2011 is the source of *Icy Trolley* (verify: p. 122). Christensen 2026 (file 05): *Streets*, wrongful opportunistic allowing without any duty. Hecht 2023 on rescue rights (verify #17).
- **Property.** Quong's unity demand (file 09 L111). Against it, Parry 2023 offers *Trolley Tool*, *Three-Track* (Quong's view makes killing three the only permissible option, L191–195), and the normative-externality argument: unilateralism and inequality (L247–259). Walen's cases *Drug Owner* and *Heirloom Tool* (Reading Notes, file 07 section). Liao & Barry on property (verify #2).
- **Structure.** Absolute within scope (Quong) vs discount (Ramakrishnan; Walen; Parfit) vs duty-matched (Tadros; Alexander; Cullity) vs multiple thresholds (Christensen).
- **Intentions.** Tadros 2020 ch. 6 (file 02) and Parry 2023 (*Costly Icy Trolley*, file 06 L107–125) favour a mental-state component, at least for omissions. Quong (file 09 L169–175, *Murders Averted* L283–313) and Ramakrishnan (*Avalanche*, *Heart Attack\**) favour objectivity. Kahn 2024 (file 08) argues against consent readings of the Mere Means Principle.
- **Loop.**
  - Thomson 1985 introduces it (verify #10). Her stipulation, quoted by Walen from the 1986 reprint (file 07 L189): the five's bodies stop the trolley if it goes straight, so it "will therefore not reach the one."
  - Otsuka 2008 argues against permitting it (verify #4). Ramakrishnan "weakly" shares the permissibility intuition but would overturn it (file 01 L249). Cullity is not confident and leans against (file 11 L253). Kamm's DTE permits it (verify #9). Walen's RCP permits the symmetric case by "interdependence" but forbids an asymmetric variant (file 07 L201–235); Costa 1987 argues similarly for the DDE (via file 07 fn 34).
  - Choo 2025 argues that no defence of the DDE or the Mere Means Principle against Loop succeeds (abstract; verify #3).
  - Hauser reports a roughly 50:50 split (via file 07 fn 32; file 11 fn 48). Liao et al. 2012 report context effects (verify #13).
- **The prerogative-to-restriction inference.**
  - Thomson 2008 (file 13, complete). *Bystander's Three Options* yields the "Third Principle": A must not kill B to save five if he can instead kill himself. She generalizes: "Since he wouldn't himself pay the cost of his good deed if he could pay it, there is no way in which he can decently regard himself as entitled to make someone else pay it" (p. 366). On this ground she rejects the bystander's permission in Switch.
  - Tadros 2011 (file 03 L85–101) and Cullity 2018 (file 11 fn 45) reply that the argument applies at most to *using*.
  - Mack 2005 argues that a prerogative's rationale is unsatisfied without restrictions (abstract; verify #12). Muñoz 2021 argues that prerogatives cannot rest on ordinary rights. Lazar 2019 grounds options and their limits in status (verify #14). Scheffler's hybrid view is the classic prerogatives-without-restrictions position.

### 2.4 Adjacent literatures the essay can draw on

- **Research ethics.**
  - Placebo controls are acceptable only where no proven treatment exists, where risks are negligible, or under conditions including that participants not "forgo treatment they would otherwise receive" (Millum & Grady 2013, search summary; verify #11).
  - The Declaration of Helsinki's placebo paragraph allows placebo only without "additional risks of serious or irreversible harm as a result of not receiving the best proven intervention" (verify #18).
  - Randomizing scarce experimental drugs is defended as fair allocation that also yields data (search 52).
  - This is the *Room*/*Heart Attack* line drawn by an institution with no stake in trolley debates.
- **Law of restitution** ("enrichment at the expense of") and takings (public use versus regulation). These are possible footnote-level analogues only; C7, C8 and C12 in `Thesis Candidates v0.1.md` explain why neither can carry the thesis.

## 3. The open niche

No one has offered a single relation that does both jobs:
1. **It sorts the cases.** It separates *Room* from *Heart Attack* and *Icy Trolley*, *Tracks* from *Loop*, *Trolley Tool* from *Life Jacket*, and *Switch with Button* from *Bridge*, and it covers omissions without presupposing rescue rights.
2. **It is explained.** It is explained by something independently credible, in a way that escapes Quong's dilemma and Ramakrishnan's arbitrariness worry.

The essay's candidate is **spending**: imposing on a person a loss that is the cost of her supplying a good to others, for the sake of that good. The criterion is fixed by an existential replacement test, with "supplying" read relative to the agent's alternatives. It is explained by the prerogative over giving: the limits of beneficence assign to each person the trade-off between what she supplies and what supplying costs her, and a restriction against spending is exactly what keeps that assignment from being hollow. Details are in `WIP Docs/Plan/Crux Memo v0.1.md`.

Why the niche is open on the evidence so far:

- **The criterion track has no such explanation.** Ramakrishnan disclaims one; Quong's is tied to use and property theory; Walen's baseline is entitlement-based (file 09 fn 27, to be confirmed against #1).
- **The rationale track has no criterion that escapes Quong's dilemma.**
  - Tadros's "compulsion" and "contribution" (file 03 L277) are the closest.
  - The essay's claim: the duty-based rationale is sound once its object is fixed as *supplying at a cost* rather than *bearing a cost for a goal*.
  - Quong's horn 1 fails for exactly this reason. It condemns Switch because Betty "is made to bear a cost that she is not duty bound to bear" (file 09 L125). But Betty supplies nothing, so no question of beneficence arises.
- **Thomson 2008 overgeneralizes the prerogative argument, and Tadros and Cullity restrict it to "using."** No one has shown that the argument's correct scope is *spending*. That scope includes omissions (*Heart Attack*, *Icy Trolley*). It excludes uses whose harm is not the price of the use (*Room*, *Trolley Tool*, *Switch with Button*, *Overdetermined*).
- **Property.** No one extends the principle to holdings by cost of supply. That extension answers both Quong's unity demand and Parry's externality argument.
- **Loop.** The symmetric treatment exists (Costa; Walen). The Spending View *derives* it, because not diverting in Thomson's own case would spend the five. It also predicts the asymmetric verdict.

## 5. Originality verdict (provisional)

**Verdict: OPEN, with a narrowed claim.** The essay should not claim:
- to be the first to link the MP to unowed service or duty (Quinn, Tadros, Alexander, Cullity);
- to be the first to restrict the prerogative argument to means-harm (Tadros, Cullity);
- the reason-blocking structure (Quong);
- the symmetric Loop verdict (Costa, Walen).

It may claim, pending the checks below:
- **(a)** The criterion: spending, via the existential replacement test relative to alternatives. This is new as the MP's content, and close to Ramakrishnan's relation for harms to persons. Say so.
- **(b)** The explanation of that criterion by the prerogative over giving, which fixes its exact shape and escapes Quong's dilemma. The novelty is the scope result and the escape, not the family of rationale.
- **(c)** The property extension by cost of supply, with its answers to Quong's bullets and Parry's objections.
- **(d)** Omissions without prior rights as a consequence, answering Parry's call for a finer difference.
- **(e)** A derivation of the symmetric/asymmetric Loop verdicts from (a) and (d).
- **(f)** The research-ethics check. No search connected the MP to the placebo standard (searches 48 and 51).

**What would close the niche:**
- #1 Walen 2016 turns out to state a cost-of-supply test.
- #5 Alexander 2016 or #7 Øverland 2014 derives the MP's scope from the limits of beneficence in this way.
- #3 Choo 2025 already refutes the derivation of symmetric-Loop permissibility from any version of the MP, in a way the essay cannot answer.

If any of these happens, fall back to Sketch B plus (c) (the property and prerogative components) or return to Stage 1.

**Significance.** High:
- It answers the field's two standing challenges, Ramakrishnan's and Quong's, at once.
- It gives verdicts on the disputed cases of the last decade.
- It turns Loop into a point of support.
- It reaches research ethics.
